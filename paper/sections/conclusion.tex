\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Small local models fail at security triage on procedure: they choose uninformative probes, stop at the wrong time, or never stop. A controller that tracks competing explanations, ranks probes by information gain per cost, accepts only evidence-backed verdicts, and can end the investigation itself repairs these failures without oracle knowledge. What to probe and when to stop fail separately, and different small models lack different parts. Once observations are discrete, the controller alone completes 0.917 of cases against 1.000 for the best configuration, and the model's remaining contributions are judging when evidence suffices and reading raw telemetry. Testing the second contribution needs data that public security datasets lack: attacks that look like routine work, and routine work that looks like an attack.
