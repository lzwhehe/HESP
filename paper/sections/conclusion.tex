\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Benchmarks for LLM security-operations agents are meant to show that an agent can investigate, and each one we audited can be partly solved without investigating: by looking up the alert a question names, by checking an attack network's address, by recalling an organization's grading policy, or by reading a tool's default name. On ExCyTIn, the agents do not take the shortcut, and their ranking survives without it; but their success changes little between questions that need one lookup and questions that need a multi-hop pivot. An investigation-free floor, benign twins for every attack, and a check that difficulty tracks the required investigation would let benchmark scores support the trust that defenders place in them.
