\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We introduced \hesp{}, a controller that holds the alert-triage procedure outside a small local LLM by splitting an investigation into what to probe next and when the evidence suffices. In three synthetic task families, controller selection and a controller-side stop address separate failures of small models, and with the verifier's evidence standard the controller alone completes every case; a pre-registered test found no gain from letting a 72B model decide when to stop. The finish guard blocks injected instructions to dismiss an alert, whereas a forged source defeats early stopping unless benign verdicts require corroboration from distinct sources. Where the model is still needed, reading raw logs, it also reopens the attack surface. Carrying these results over to real telemetry requires paired, objectively labeled data.
