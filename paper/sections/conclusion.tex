\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We introduced \hesp{}, a controller that holds the alert-triage procedure outside a small local LLM by splitting an investigation into what to probe next and when the evidence suffices. In two synthetic task families, controller selection and a controller-side stop address separate failures of small models, and with the verifier's evidence standard the controller alone completes every case, so the value of an LLM over such a controller remains to be shown. The finish guard blocks injected instructions to dismiss an alert, whereas a forged source defeats early stopping unless benign verdicts require corroboration from distinct sources. Carrying these results over to raw telemetry, where the model would have to produce the observations itself, requires paired, objectively labeled data.
